Emergency Vehicle Priority Simulation

Preempts normal cycle: emergency road served first with max green (60s); remaining roads share 60s by density without changing vehicle counts
STANDBY

Emergency priority acts as a dispatch flag. Vehicle counts, density levels, and traffic shares remain strictly as measured. The designated corridor receives 60s green immediately; remaining approaches share (120s − 60s = 60s) proportionally to their traffic density (min 10s). When the priority cycle concludes, normal adaptive cycling resumes automatically.
